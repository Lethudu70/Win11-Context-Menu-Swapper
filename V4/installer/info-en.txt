CONTEXT MENU SWAPPER v4.0 — LETH EDITION

Your Windows menu, on your terms.

• Rounded Qt 6 interface in French and English.
• Automatic Windows version detection.
• Verified configuration, backup and undo.
• Explorer restarts only when you request it.

PERSONAL INSTALLATION
The app and its DLLs are installed for your account. Installation does not change your context-menu configuration.

EXPERIMENTAL MODE
HKCU is the default mode. HKLM can affect all accounts: it remains experimental, is not recommended for standard users, and requires UAC authorization and confirmations inside the app. Test it in Windows Sandbox or a virtual machine.

YOUR DATA STAYS WITH YOU
Uninstall removes the program and its shortcuts. It preserves preferences, backups and your Windows menu configuration.
